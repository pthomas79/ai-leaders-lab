Step 3 · Conversation

Conversation: let Copilot interview you

Answer its questions (up to 6), then check its playback.

1
Answer briefly and specifically
Real details beat polished wording.
2
Confirm or correct the playback
Don't move on until Copilot has your situation right.
Why this matters

Instead of guessing what you need from a single prompt, Copilot builds a shared understanding of your situation. That's the difference between using AI as a search engine and using it as a thinking partner. The playback is where you catch misalignment before it produces anything.

Step 4 · Refine

Refine: sharpen it together

Push back and challenge the thinking before you ask for the file.

1
Ask Copilot:
“What assumption in this is most likely to be wrong?”
2
Move on when you'd defend it
Ready means the thinking is right, not just the wording.
More questions to try
  • How could this be better?
  • Let's roast this content we've been creating together
  • Now that we have the full picture, what are some tensions, gaps, or opportunities to simplify?
  • Thoughts?
  • What else should I consider?
Step 5 · Produce

Produce: get your deliverable

Paste this. Copilot proposes a structure; agree or change it, then it produces the file.

Your second prompt
Not perfect?

That's expected. Keep refining in the chat ("Make the tone more formal"), or open the file in Word, Excel or PowerPoint and use Copilot there to edit in place. You can also ask "How can we make this better?"

⚡ Bonus prompt: stress-test it before you share
Review what you just created with a critical eye. What's missing? What could be stronger? What would a skeptical executive push back on? Give me your top 3 suggestions for improvement.
🎯

You just learned how to pilot Copilot.

From a blank screen to a real deliverable. Here's what you did:

You own the final output. Review it before you use it.

  • Context — you told Copilot what you need, who it's for, and what good looks like
  • Conversation — Copilot interviewed you and played back its understanding
  • Refine — you challenged and sharpened the thinking together
  • Produce — Copilot proposed a structure, you agreed it, and it produced the asset
🔑
Use the same four moves everywhere: Context, Conversation, Refine, Produce. In Teams, Word, Outlook or PowerPoint.
🧠 Pro move: save a summary for next time

Before you close the conversation, ask Copilot: "Summarise this conversation and its outcomes so I can use it as a starting point next time." Save that summary in a Word document or in OneNote. Next time, drop that document into the chat and Copilot picks up where you left off.
