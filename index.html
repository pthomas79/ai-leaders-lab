Step 3 · Conversation

Conversation: let Copilot interview you

This is a back-and-forth conversation, not a questionnaire. The goal is alignment: Copilot understands your situation and you feel understood.

1
Answer its questions
Copilot will ask you up to 6 questions, one at a time. Answer briefly and specifically. Real details beat polished wording.
2
Check its playback
Copilot then plays back what it has understood. Confirm it, or correct anything that's off. Don't move on until it has your situation right.
💡
Why this matters: Instead of guessing what you need from a single prompt, Copilot builds a shared understanding of your situation. That's the difference between using AI as a search engine and using it as a thinking partner. The playback is where you catch misalignment before it produces anything.
Step 4 · Refine

Refine: sharpen it together

Don't rush to the output. Push back, challenge, and sharpen the thinking with Copilot first.

1
Challenge the thinking
Try questions like:
  • How could this be better?
  • Let's roast this content we've been creating together
  • What assumption in this is most likely to be wrong?
  • Now that we have the full picture, what are some tensions, gaps, or opportunities to simplify?
  • Thoughts?
  • What else should I consider?
2
Move on when the thinking is right
You're ready when you'd be comfortable defending the content, not just when the wording sounds good.

🎯

You just learned how to pilot Copilot.

In under 15 minutes, you went from a blank screen to a real deliverable. Here's what you did:

  • Context — you told Copilot what you need, who it's for, and what good looks like
  • Conversation — Copilot interviewed you and played back its understanding
  • Refine — you challenged and sharpened the thinking together
  • Produce — Copilot proposed a structure, you agreed it, and it produced the asset
🔑
The method you just learned applies everywhere. Next time you use Copilot in Teams, Word, Outlook, or PowerPoint, follow the same four moves: Context, Conversation, Refine, Produce. You'll get dramatically better results than prompting from scratch.
🧠 Pro Move
Save a summary so you can pick up where you left off
Before you close the conversation, ask Copilot: "Summarise this conversation and its outcomes so I can use it as a starting point next time." Save that summary in a Word document or in OneNote. Next time you start a new Copilot session on this topic, just drop that document into the chat. Copilot will pick up where you left off, with all the context from today. Think of it as a briefing note for your future self.
